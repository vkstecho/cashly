Account & Data Deletion

Request account deletion

Email Cashly Support from your registered email address, or provide your registered phone number so support can verify account ownership. Never include your PIN, OTP, or a copy of your financial records.

Contact Cashly Support

Support will explain the verification process and the timing for removal of Firebase account and cloud records. Export a backup first if you want a copy of your data.

To remove records cached on this device after verification, sign out and clear Cashly’s site data or native app storage on every device where you used Cashly.
