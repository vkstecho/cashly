Welcome to Cashly — Track every rupee with clarity

Money in India moves fast — salary, UPI, cards, EMIs. Cashly helps you see it clearly without spreadsheet pain.

Cashly is built for one job: help you understand where your money goes, and what is still safe to spend this month.

On the home screen you get a spend ring, budget context, and a simple “safe to spend per day” number. History, categories and merchants help you review without noise.

What you can do today

  • Log expenses in seconds
  • Import bank/UPI SMS (paste on web; native on Android app)
  • Set a monthly budget and watch the ring
  • Open Weekly summary for a calm review

This blog will share practical money systems for India — salary day routines, credit card hygiene, and simple frameworks that actually stick.
